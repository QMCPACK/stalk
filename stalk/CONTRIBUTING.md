# CONTRIBUTING to STALK

Contributions to STALK are welcome! The algorithms, their implementations and the
documentation are in active development, and we are happy to receive feedback, bug reports,
and code contributions.

Feel free to open issues or pull requests on GitHub, or contact the authors directly
[by email](mailto:tiihonen@iki.fi) One of the most effective contributions is to check in
your intent to use or adapt the code, ask for guidance and report back on successes and
difficulties.

Welcome tokens of support include citing the original work, acknowledging the use of STALK
in your work, following the project in GitHub and rating the project with a star.
